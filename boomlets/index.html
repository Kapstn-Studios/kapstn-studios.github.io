Boomlets: a pod blast puzzle game for Android

A cozy 3D puzzle arcade game about Pip, a little sprout with a big fizz. Drop a fizzy pod, step back and watch it pop in a plus-shaped blast that breaks crates, sets off other pods and sends critters packing. Clear 25 floating worlds, each with its own twist and a boss at the end.

  • 150 levels
  • 25 worlds
  • 25 bosses
  • 450 stars
  • One-finger controls
  • Plays offline
Get it on Google Play

How a level works

Tap a tile and Pip walks there. Double-tap to drop a fizzy pod. A moment later it pops in a plus-shaped blast: crates break, other pods in reach go off too, and critters in its path take a hit. Pop every critter to open the exit, then get there before the four-minute clock runs out.

Crates hide power-ups, and finishing fast earns up to three stars. Levels are short, so one fits a coffee break.

25 worlds, a new twist in each

Every world has six levels, its own look and music, and a rule you won't have seen before:

  • Meadow Isles: the basics, then conveyor belts, barrels and warp pads.
  • Candy Coast: tide pools that flood and drain.
  • Cocoa Canyon: chocolate mud and cracked tiles that crumble behind you.
  • Frost Peaks: slippery ice and frozen crates.
  • Neon Nightway: coloured light gates and the switches that flip them.
  • Clockwork Keep: turnstiles that turn when a blast hits them.
  • Prism Caverns: mirrors that bend blasts and prisms that split them.
  • Gale Heights: wind gusts that push everything one way.
  • Ember Forge: lava vents that fire on their own.
  • Bramblewood: bramble hedges that grow back.
  • Spooky Hollow: jack-o'-lanterns that blast back.
  • Toybox Town: springboards that bounce you over walls.
  • Rhythm Cloudway: cloud platforms that swap on the beat.
  • Magnet Mines: magnets that pull and push pods.
  • Origami Valley: paper panels that fold over like a page.
  • Inkwell Library: book stacks to push around.
  • Paint Plaza: paint tubs that colour your blasts.
  • Domino Dunes: lines of toppling dominoes.
  • Slime Lab: slimes that split when you blast them.
  • Pixel Arcade: wrap-around edges, like an old arcade screen.
  • Echo Grotto: shells that store a blast and let it go later.
  • Comet Orbit: asteroids that circle a planet.
  • Seasons Grove: spring, summer, autumn and winter, each changing the board.
  • Mirror Marsh: a mirrored shadow twin that copies every move.
  • Starlight Summit: light the stars to build bridges across the sky.

A boss at the end of every world

The sixth level of each world is a boss battle: 25 big, silly bosses, from Thornback and Captain Clawdia the giant crab to Sir Cuckoo, the Origami Dragon, King Slime and, at the very top, the Star Owl.

Power-ups

  • Extra pods and longer blasts.
  • Speed boots, and a kick to send pods sliding.
  • A shield and extra hearts.
  • A rare Pod Party: drop five pods at once.

Easy to start, friendly to every player

  • One-finger controls: tap to walk, double-tap to drop a pod.
  • A guided first level and a How to play page.
  • A large text option.
  • Colour-blind help: danger tiles get stripes, so they never rely on colour alone.
  • Vibration, sound and music can each be turned off.
  • No account needed, and your progress is saved on your phone.

Ads

Boomlets is free to play with ads. Ads between levels start only after you have cleared four levels, and then play at most once every three levels. If you run out of lives you can choose to watch a short ad for an extra one, and you can watch one to open a new world before you have the stars for it. There are no banner ads.

Good to know

Does Boomlets work offline?

Yes. It plays offline with no account, so it works without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

How do the controls work?

One finger is enough: tap a tile to walk there and double-tap to drop a pod.

How many levels are there?

150 levels across 25 worlds, six per world, with a boss battle at the end of each world and 450 stars to collect.

Is it a puzzle game or an arcade game?

Both. Each level is a small puzzle about where to drop your pods, played in real time with critters on the move and a clock running.

Is Boomlets free?

Yes, it is free on Google Play, and no account is needed. It is supported by ads: now and then a short ad between levels, and optional ads for an extra life or to open a world early.
