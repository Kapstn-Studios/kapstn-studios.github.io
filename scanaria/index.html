Scanaria: OCR app and document scanner for Android

A text scanner app for Android. Turn photos into text, scan documents to searchable PDFs, translate what you scan and ask your documents questions. Text recognition runs on your phone with Google ML Kit, and scanning text works offline.

  • Image to text
  • Offline OCR
  • Scan to PDF
  • Translate
  • QR codes
  • On-device AI
Get it on Google Play

Image to text (OCR)

  • Extract text from any photo or image.
  • Reads Latin, Chinese, Devanagari, Japanese and Korean scripts.
  • Live OCR: point your camera and read text as you move.
  • Text scanning works offline, and no account is needed.

Document scanner and scan to PDF

  • Built-in document scanner with edge detection.
  • Save scans as searchable PDFs: the image plus a selectable, invisible text layer.
  • Copy, share or save as .txt, PDF, Markdown, CSV or vCard (.vcf).

Receipts, business cards and tables

  • Finds emails, phone numbers, links, dates and prices. Tap one to copy, call, open or save it.
  • Recognises receipts, business cards and invoices and pulls out their fields.
  • Approximate table detection turns rows and columns into a clean grid, with CSV export.
  • Redact emails and phone numbers in an image before you share it.

Translate what you scan

Translate recognised text on your phone, with automatic language detection. Each language pack is a one-time download of about 30 MB.

Barcodes and QR codes

Scan barcodes and QR codes, or make your own QR codes for links, emails and phone numbers.

Ask your documents

After a one-time model download (1.1 to 1.6 GB, depending on the model), a language model running on your phone answers questions about your documents, and your documents are not sent to any server.

Find any scan again

A full scan history with tags for fast filtering and search. Revisit, re-export or re-translate any past scan.

Private by design

Your photos, scans and extracted text are processed on your phone and are not uploaded to our servers. All features are currently free.

Good to know

How do I copy text from an image on Android?

Open Scanaria and take a photo or open an image. It extracts the text with on-device OCR, and you can copy it, share it or save it as a file.

Does Scanaria work offline?

Text scanning works offline. Translation needs a one-time download for each language (about 30 MB), and the AI assistant needs a one-time model download (1.1 to 1.6 GB). After that, both run on your phone.

Can it scan documents to PDF on Android?

Yes. The document scanner finds the page edges and saves searchable PDFs, so you can search and select the text inside them.

Can it scan business cards, receipts and QR codes?

Yes. It recognises business cards (export them as vCard contacts), receipts and invoices, and it scans barcodes and QR codes.

Is it a photo translator?

Yes. Scan a menu, sign or document and translate the text on your phone. The language is detected automatically.

Is Scanaria free?

Yes. All features are currently free, and no account is needed to scan.
