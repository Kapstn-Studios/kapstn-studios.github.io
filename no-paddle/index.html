No Paddle: a sailing boat game for Android

A squall snapped your oar, so only the wind can carry you home. No Paddle is a sailing game about reading the wind: pick your angle, trim the sail, tack upwind and ride the gusts home without capsizing, hitting a reef or getting pulled into a whirlpool.

  • 110 voyages
  • 11 worlds
  • Daily Voyage
  • Regattas
  • No in-app purchases
Get it on Google Play

Sail like a real sailor

You can't row, and you can't sail straight into the wind. The game is built on the real moves:

  • Point into the wind and the sail just flaps. Zig-zag upwind about 45° off it, and keep your speed through each tack.
  • A beam reach is the fastest way to sail. Dark, ruffled water is a gust; pale, glassy water is a lull or an island's wind shadow.
  • Gusts heel the boat over. Ease the sail or bear away before you go over.
  • Stuck head to wind? Dolphins surface on the side to turn to and swim off the way to go.

110 voyages across 11 worlds

The first ten voyages, from Lost Oar to Pirate Waters, bring squalls with rain and lightning, a night fog with something in the water, ice floes drifting across the channel and a pirate brig that is fast downwind but can't sail against it. Then come ten more worlds of ten voyages each:

  • Volcano Isles: lava flows, pumice rafts and vents that boil and then blow.
  • Harbor City: container quays, swinging cranes and ferries crossing your course.
  • Planet Zeta: a violet sea of crystal isles, fungus forests and giant jellies.
  • Jungle Delta: mangroves, macaws, hippos and a crocodile in its pool.
  • Dino Lagoon: sauropods in the ferns, pterosaurs over red cliffs and a plesiosaur.
  • Sunken City: drowned streets, rowing galleys and an eel in the city gate.
  • Ghost Sea: green lanterns, ghost ships in the mist and a wraith.
  • Aurora Ice: northern lights, polar bears, drifting icebergs and an orca.
  • Toy Bay: brick islands, sandcastles, rubber ducks and a jack-in-the-box.
  • Sky Sea: cloud banks, floating isles with waterfalls, airships and thunderclouds.

More ways to sail

  • Daily Voyage: a new course every day, the same for everyone. Sail it on consecutive days to build a streak.
  • Open Sea: an endless run east against the clock. Sail through the buoy gates to win back time.
  • Regattas: race three rival boats round the marks, from the Club fleet up to the Championship.
  • The Shipyard: five sailing boats, up to a three-masted galleon, each with its own speed, pointing, stability and toughness.
  • Bring castaways home, race the ghost of your best run, win 35 medals and hunt for rare black pearls.

Play your way

Touch anywhere and slide your thumb to steer, pinch to zoom, or use a keyboard. Colour-blind friendly colours, reduced motion, three text sizes, left-handed controls and adjustable steering.

No in-app purchases and no account needed. Your progress is saved on your phone.
