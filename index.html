Tiny CourierNEW

A relaxing 3D puzzle game

A calm, cozy logic puzzle game for Android. A little walking mailbox has parcels to deliver: turn tiny floating clay islands, slide bridges, ride lifts and bring every parcel to its pad. No timers, no lives, no rush, and the puzzles play offline.

  • 460 levels
  • 7 worlds
  • Offline puzzles
  • Hints and undo
  • Colour-blind friendly
Get it on Google Play

Scanaria

OCR app & document scanner

Every scan tool in one Android app. Turn photos into text with on-device OCR, scan documents to searchable PDFs, translate a menu through your camera, ask your documents questions and edit PDFs. Text scanning works offline; translation and the AI assistant need a one-time download.

  • Image to text
  • Offline OCR
  • Scan to PDF
  • Photo translate
  • PDF editor
  • On-device AI
Get it on Google Play
More about Scanaria

No Paddle

A sailing boat game

A sailing game for Android about reading the wind. Your oar is gone, so tack upwind, trim the sail, outsail pirates and sail home through storms, whirlpools and kraken fog. Race regattas, sail the Daily Voyage and take five boats, up to a three-masted galleon.

  • 110 voyages
  • 11 worlds
  • Daily voyage
  • Regattas
Get it on Google Play
