Tiny Courier: a relaxing 3D puzzle game for Android

A calm logic puzzle game about a little walking mailbox with parcels to deliver. Each level is a small puzzle on a floating island of soft clay: turn it around, walk to a spot, and carry every parcel to the pad of the same colour.

  • 460 levels
  • 7 worlds
  • No timers, no lives
  • Offline puzzles
  • Hints and undo
Get it on Google Play

How a level works

Drag to turn the island and tap a spot to walk there. The orange parts move: slide a bridge into place, ride a lift, press a button to open a gate, hop on a conveyor belt or turn a crank to swing a turntable around. Work out the order and the way opens. When every pad is filled, the flag at the exit goes up.

It is a gentle brain teaser rather than a test of speed. Every level has been checked by a solver, so there is always a way through.

Seven worlds, 460 levels

  • Meadow: hills, stairs, sliding bridges and rafts.
  • Harbour: lifts, gates, buttons and conveyor belts.
  • Old Town: two parcels at once and turning bridges.
  • Orchard: wooden planks that hold for just one crossing.
  • Blossom Valley: spring gardens where every moving part comes together.
  • Sunflower Fields: springs that bounce you over walls and gaps.
  • Frost Peaks: the final climb, over thin ice that cracks and breaks behind you.

Each world's levels wind up a map of floating islands, with windmills, a lighthouse and a hot-air balloon to tap along the way.

Play at your own pace

  • No timers and no lives.
  • Undo any move, or restart the level.
  • Stuck? A hint shows the next step of the shortest way to finish.
  • Earn up to three stars by finishing in fewer moves.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone.

A gentle look and sound

  • Hand-pressed clay blocks, a dusk sky and a sea of clouds.
  • Calm music for each world, soft sound effects and light vibration (you can turn them off).
  • Graphics settings for older phones.

Ads, kept calm

The first 20 levels have no ads at all. After that, you can choose to watch a short video to turn on hints for a level, and now and then a short ad plays between levels. Never in the middle of a puzzle, and never banners.

No account needed, and the puzzles play offline.

Good to know

Does Tiny Courier work offline?

Yes. The puzzles play offline, so it works as a game without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

What makes it relaxing?

No timers, no lives and no rush. You can undo any move, each world has its own calm music, and every level can be solved.

Is it a 3D puzzle game?

Yes. Each level is a small 3D island of clay that you turn around with your finger to find the way.

How many levels are there?

460 logic puzzle levels across seven worlds, from Meadow to Frost Peaks.

Is Tiny Courier free?

Yes, it is free on Google Play, and no account is needed. The first 20 levels have no ads; after that a short ad plays now and then between levels, never in the middle of a puzzle and never as a banner.
